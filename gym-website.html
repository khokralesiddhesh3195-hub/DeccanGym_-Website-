Coaching over hype

Deccan Strength Co. started as a single squat rack in a Kothrud garage. Nine years on, it's still built around the same idea: progress comes from consistent, well-coached work, not from chasing the next trend to walk through the door.

We measure the gym by how our members move at fifty, not how they look at twenty-five.
